                Benchmark
                
      
                
    
                

    
                
      
                
        
                
          
                How long does a meeting take?
                
          
                Runs your selected models over five minutes of audio the way
            a meeting runs them, and estimates how long an hour of meeting takes on
            this machine. Where the summary can run on the GPU or the CPU, both are
            timed and you can switch to the faster one. Takes a few minutes;
            recordings added meanwhile wait until it is done.
                
        
                

        
                
          
          
        
                
        
                
        
                

        
                
          
                
          
                
        
                

        
                
          
            
                StageModelOnTimeSpeed
                
                          
          
        
                

        
                
          
                
          
        
                

        
                
          How the test works
          
                  
            
                • Audio — Run benchmark uses five minutes of
              synthetic, speech-like noise: none of your recordings are read.
              Parakeet and the speaker models spend the same compute on it as on
              speech. Whisper, Qwen3-ASR and Cohere Transcribe write text token by
              token and spend almost nothing on noise, so they are timed only with
              Run on one of your recordings (its first five minutes,
              decoded on this computer, never uploaded).
                  • 
            
                • Transcription and speaker separation — run
              together with the same thread split and silence removal as a
              meeting.
                  • 
            
                • Voice matching — one voiceprint per separated
              speaker.
                  • 
            
                • Summary — the selected summary model reads a
              2048-token transcript and writes up to 384 tokens. Its reading and
              writing speeds are scaled to an hour of meeting (about 12,600
              transcript tokens and a 600-token answer).
                  • 
          
                
          
                Not included: decoding the recording file (seconds for
            most files) and the extra summary passes for meetings longer than about
            90 minutes.
